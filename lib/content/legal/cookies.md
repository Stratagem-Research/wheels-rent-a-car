# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-15. [draft by counsel]_

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences over time.

## 2. Categories we use

- **Essential cookies** — required for the site to work (authentication, search-state persistence, booking funnel). Cannot be disabled.
- **Analytics cookies** — anonymised data we use to improve the site (Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel). Opt in via the cookie banner.
- **Marketing cookies** — used to personalise advertising on other platforms. Opt in via the cookie banner.

## 3. Managing cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit, and update your preferences at any time from the footer "Cookie preferences" link.

Most browsers also let you delete or block cookies entirely. Doing so may prevent parts of the site from working.

## 4. Third-party cookies

We use the following third parties, each with their own privacy policies:

- Google (Analytics, Maps)
- Meta (Pixel)
- Areeba (Payment Services)
- WhatsApp (Business API)
